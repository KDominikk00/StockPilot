Privacy policy

Last updated: November 2026

This page explains how the StockPilot website handles your data when you join the launch waitlist. It is a short, plain-language notice and is not legal advice.

What we collect

  • Your email address.
  • The language you chose on the site (EN or PL) and the time you signed up.
  • A short-lived, hashed form of your IP address, used only to limit repeated sign-ups. The raw address is not stored.

Why, and on what basis

We use your email only to tell you when StockPilot is available on the App Store. The legal basis is your consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR), which you give by ticking the box on the form. You can withdraw it at any time.

Who handles it

The site is hosted by Vercel Inc., and the waitlist is stored in a managed Redis database (Upstash). They process data on our behalf. These providers may process data outside the European Economic Area; please see their own privacy policies for details. We do not sell or share your email with anyone else.

How long we keep it

Until we have sent the launch message, or until you ask us to delete it, whichever comes first.

Your rights

You can ask for access to your data, correction, deletion, or to withdraw your consent at any time. You also have the right to complain to your data protection authority (in Poland: the President of the Personal Data Protection Office, UODO).

Analytics

We use Vercel Web Analytics to count page views and visitors. It does not use cookies and does not track you across sites.

Contact

To exercise your rights or ask a question, email dominikkowalski331@gmail.com.

Not investment advice

StockPilot is an informational app and nothing on this site is investment advice.
